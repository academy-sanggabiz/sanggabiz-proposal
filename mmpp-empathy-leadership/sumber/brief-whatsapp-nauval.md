> 📣 TRAINING PROVIDER / TRAINER OPPORTUNITY – PT Makassar Mega Putra Prima
>
> PT Makassar Mega Putra Prima (PT MMPP) is looking for a professional training provider or independent trainer for an upcoming private, in-house training program planned for 2027. We are looking for a training program with the following details:
>
> 📒 Training Topic: Communication & Teamwork Leadership Based on Empathy
> 🎯 Training Objectives: To strengthen leaders' communication skills, collaborative mindset, and leadership capabilities through an empathy-based approach.
> 📝 Training Details:
> - Format: Offline, private training exclusively for PT MMPP (not combined with participants from other companies)
> - Tentative Schedule: Q1 2027 (January–March)
> - Location: Makassar or Jakarta, Indonesia
> - Participants: PT MMPP employees only
>
> Note:
> For Training Providers / Vendors:
> We would appreciate proposals that include the training program, trainer profile, methodology, estimated cost, and training venue/location. Ideally, we are looking for an all-in-one package that includes both the training program and venue, so we can consider the overall offering.
>
> For Independent Trainers:
> Please share your training profile, relevant experience, proposed materials or learning approach, and estimated fee. PT MMPP will arrange the training venue, with Makassar and Jakarta as possible locations.
